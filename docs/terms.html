Shape it up (To-Primitive) · Last updated: October 21, 2026

1. What the app is

Shape it up is a personal tool that creates short videos made of geometric shapes from photos and videos, and publishes them to the user's own YouTube and TikTok accounts. It runs locally on the user's own computer; there is no hosted service, no sign-up and no payment.

2. Who can use it
