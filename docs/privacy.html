Privacy Policy

Shape it up (To-Primitive) · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Summary

Shape it up runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, and does not sell or share any data. The only data it handles is what it needs to publish the user's videos to the user's own accounts.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the user's permission, the scopes to upload videos (youtube.upload) and to read the channel name (youtube.readonly), only to show which channel is connected.
  • TikTok: with the user's permission, basic profile information (user.info.basic) to show which account is connected, and permission to post videos (video.publish).

Where it is stored

The access tokens granted by YouTube and TikTok are stored only on the user's computer, in a local file of the app, and are used only to publish the videos the user puts in the queue. Videos, titles and descriptions stay on the user's computer until they are published. Nothing is sent to the authors of the app or to any party other than YouTube or TikTok, and only when publishing.

How to remove access

Use "Disconnect" in the app to delete the stored tokens, and revoke the app's access at any time from your Google account permissions or from TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions).

Third parties

Published videos are handled by YouTube and TikTok under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy and TikTok Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly process children's data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the project's GitHub page.

Política de privacidad

Shape it up corre por completo en la computadora del usuario: no tiene servidores propios, no recolecta estadísticas y no vende ni comparte datos. Con el permiso del usuario, accede a su cuenta de YouTube (subir videos y leer el nombre del canal) y de TikTok (perfil básico y publicar videos). Los permisos (tokens) se guardan solo en la computadora del usuario y se usan solo para publicar los videos que él pone en la cola. "Desconectar" en la app los borra, y el acceso se puede revocar en cualquier momento desde la configuración de la cuenta de Google o de TikTok. Los videos publicados quedan bajo las políticas de YouTube y TikTok. Consultas: página del proyecto en GitHub.
